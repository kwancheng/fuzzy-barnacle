## Project

"Fuzzy Barnacle" is an iOS app (SwiftUI + SwiftData), deployment target iOS 26.5. It is a **generative art piece**: a body of water, and the lives on and in it.

- **The water**: a tide drives the motes, the light shafts, and the plume sway. On the water's own 1440 s clock there is a day and a night (`ContentView.daylight`); in the night the colony is lit by its own faint glow, and the hand is the only lamp there is, unless the moon is crossing. The sky's light from above has a **color**, not only a brightness: it comes down **warm gold at the water's turning** (the low light) and **white at the high** (`ContentView.skyWarmth` = `exp(−((daylight−0.30)/0.22)²/2)` — a warmth that peaks at the turning, where the light is low, and is gone at the high and nearly gone in the deep night; `ContentView.skyLightRGB`/`skyLightColor` map it to a color, warm gold `(1.0, 0.66, 0.52)` at the turning, white at the high). `drawWater` feeds that color to everything the sky's light touches — the soft surface light, the two light shafts, and the motes (all were plain white; at the high they still render white exactly as before, so a full day is unchanged). The piece's own small lights keep their own colors (the deep's pale blue, the twin's paler blue, the colony's cold glow, the wake's cyan) — only the sky's light now carries a hue. And the caption now **names the turning** (`waterLine` returns "the water is in its turning" in the [0.25, 0.75] band, where it was once silent): "the water is in its night" (light < 0.25), "the water is in its turning" (0.25–0.75), "the water is in its day" (light > 0.75). A pure function of t — the sky does not remember the turning; verify with the shifted clock. And **the water itself drinks the sky's color** (`ContentView.waterColor(t, light:)` — the water's own base depth gradient is multiplied by `skyLightRGB(t)`: at the high the light is white and the water's own blue is exactly as it was, so a full day is unchanged; at the turning the warm light gilds it, the way the sea is gilded at dusk — copper where the light lies, teal where it does not; in the deep night the sky has little light left to give a color, and the water is nearly dark. The water only drinks — its channels never exceed the color of the water with no sky's color in it — and when the turning turns to the day the water is the water again, and does not remember the gold).
- **The colony**: barnacles settle on a tap (persisted as `Barnacle` in SwiftData), accrete to adult size, and age. Holding one prys it off, leaving a `Ghost` — a trace that holds, then dissolves and is pruned. The colony has a word in its calm — **the opening**: the tuck is the colony's word in its weather (the closing, under the storm), and its home is the calm — the water is calm most of the water's days — so it has one at home: one soft chime, sparse, at the colony's own pace, full in the deep calm and gone at the storm's full (see "The voice"). And when a new life completes its 60 s of accretion the piece *witnesses its becoming*: a bloom of the colony's own light — one slow ring that goes out once and is gone in ~6 s (`ContentView.bloomFade`; the witness window is `ContentView.isBecoming`, [60, 75) s — after it, the creature is what it is, and the piece keeps its size, not its becoming) — and the colony's one opening voice opens for it, a chime, the loudest the opening is (`ContentView.bloomChime` = 0.03·e^(−age·1.1), gone in ~4 s, still below the closing's full 0.06: the colony's quiet word keeps below its storm word, the way the sky's word keeps below the body's); the witness is kept only while the bloom lasts (ephemeral `bloomed`/`blooms` state, pruned in `speak()`), and forgotten, the way the water forgets everything. And the water knows the comings and the goings — the one knowing the piece keeps of the hand's works: when a new life settles the water knows it has come (`ContentView.comingGain` = 0.008·e^(−age·0.5) — the coming's grain, see "The voice"), and when a life is pried off the water knows it has gone (`ContentView.goingGain` = 0.005·e^(−age·0.35) — a little smaller than the coming's, and lingering a little longer, the way a loss lingers a moment longer than a coming): the knowing is kept a moment in `speak()`'s ephemeral `witnesses` state (a `.coming`/`.going` witness stamped in world time, the way the hand's other answers run on world time, not the shifted clock — the caption reads them on the world's now, the way the voice does), pruned after 12 s, and forgotten, the way the water forgets everything: the water keeps the body and the trace, and not the knowing. The knowings keep below the colony's quiet word — the coming's full 0.008 < the opening's full 0.015, the going's 0.005 < the coming's, the two together 0.013 < the opening's full, all below the closing's full 0.06 — the water's knowing keeps below the colony's word, the way the piece keeps its own account of its own voices. The caption names the moments themselves (`comingLine`: "a new life has come to the water" while a coming witness is under 8 s; `goingLine`: "a life has left the water" while a going witness is under 12 s — the count counts the staying, and these name the moment of the coming and the moment of the going, which the count does not name), and a moment later the caption keeps its silence, the way the caption keeps its silence about the water itself.
- **The passing**: five "drifters" ride the current and never settle (`drifterRawPosition` — deterministic from the water's clock, stored nowhere). And the quick ones have a voice at last — **the skitter** (see "The voice"): the quick ones' feet on the water's surface, riding the current the way the quick ones ride the current — the flood and the ebb carry them more than the slack, a little more in the night (the way the quick ones shine of their own where the light is gone), more in the storm (the way the quick ones ride the storm), and never fully stopped: at the slack the skitter thins to its floor, less, not none. ## Layout

- `Fuzzy Barnacle/` — app target source (note the space in the directory name; the Swift module is `Fuzzy_Barnacle`, hence `@testable import Fuzzy_Barnacle` in tests)
- `Fuzzy Barnacle.xcodeproj` — single project; one scheme: `Fuzzy Barnacle`; targets: `Fuzzy Barnacle`, `Fuzzy BarnacleTests` (Swift Testing), `Fuzzy BarnacleUITests` (XCUITest)
- Bundle IDs use the `com.gk.` prefix

## Commands

All commands run from the repo root; the project path contains a space, so quote it.

```sh
# Build (Release is the default config; use -configuration Debug for faster iteration)
xcodebuild -project "Fuzzy Barnacle.xcodeproj" -scheme "Fuzzy Barnacle" \
  -destination 'generic/platform=iOS Simulator' build

# Run all unit tests (pick a concrete simulator from: xcrun simctl list devices available)
xcodebuild -project "Fuzzy Barnacle.xcodeproj" -scheme "Fuzzy Barnacle" \
  -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 17' test

# Run a single test (class/method in Fuzzy_BarnacleTests)
xcodebuild -project "Fuzzy Barnacle.xcodeproj" -scheme "Fuzzy Barnacle" \
  -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 17' \
  -only-testing:'Fuzzy BarnacleTests/Fuzzy_BarnacleTests/example' test
```

Prefer the Xcode app / simulator for interactive work; `xcodebuild` is for scriptable builds and tests.
